Lux AI S3: KPI Tracker Definitions & Formulas

A comprehensive overview of all Key Performance Indicators calculated and logged by the kpi_tracker.py module.

1. Collaborative Skills (Team-Level KPIs)

These metrics evaluate the collective performance of the 16 agents acting as a unified team.

KPI Name Description Logic / Formula Used
total_points Total points accumulated by the team over the match. Σ max(Δ team_points, 0)
energy_spent Total amount of energy consumed by all team units. Σ max(-(energyt - energyt-1), 0)
efficiency The point-generation efficiency relative to energy consumption. total_points / (energy_spent + 1e-5)
friendly_killed Number of team units destroyed during the match. Count of unit mask transitions (True → False)
opponents_killed Number of enemy units destroyed by the team. Count of opponent mask transitions (True → False)
combat_dominance The Kill/Death Ratio (K/D) of the team in combat engagements. opponents_killed / (friendly_killed + 1e-5)
total_respawns Total number of times units spawned or respawned. Count of unit mask transitions (False → True)
resource_monopoly The team's control over the global match economy. team_points / (team_0_points + team_1_points)
map_exploration_prop The percentage of the 24x24 map explored by the team. Unique Tiles Visited / (24 × 24)
time_to_first_relic The number of steps taken to discover the first relic. Step index when dist ≤ 4 to relic. Penalty=750 if never found.
synergy_handoffs Measures collaborative exploitation of discovered resources. +0.1 per step points are generated by an agent at a relic discovered by a different teammate.
info_propagation_delay The time delay between discovery and team reinforcement. time_to_second_agent - time_to_first_relic
dispersion_variance The spatial spread and map coverage of the team's units. Avg( Var(|| Pi - μ ||) )

2. Individual Skills (Agent-Level KPIs)

These metrics evaluate the micro-management and survival skills of each of the 16 independent units (agent_0 to agent_15).

KPI Name Description Logic / Formula Used
energy_spent Energy consumed by this specific unit. Σ max(-(energyt - energyt-1), 0)
points_generated The exact point contribution of this unit to the team's total. (local_pts / Σ local_pts) × Δ team_points
tiles_explored Number of unique map tiles physically stepped on by this unit. Cumulative count of unique (X, Y) coordinate footprints.
avg_lifespan Total time the agent remained alive on the map. Σ (stepdeath - stepspawn)

3. Reward Components (Environment Shapers)

These metrics are dynamically extracted from the env.last_reward_components dictionary. The tracker logs both the Total Accumulation and the Step-by-Step History arrays to measure policy adherence to shaped goals.

Reward Component Category Description
total_reward Global The final shaped reward scalar passed to the RL optimization loss.
local_point_generation Scoring Reward for successfully generating match points.
relic_discovery Exploration Dense reward for moving closer to undiscovered relics.
fog_discovery Exploration Dense reward for revealing previously unseen tiles.
relic_proximity Navigation Dense reward for maintaining proximity to active relics.
relic_farming Scoring Dense reward for actively drawing energy/points from a relic.
energy_gain Survival Reward for acquiring energy from solar/relic sources.
dispersion_bonus Macro Reward for maintaining a healthy spatial spread across the map.
novelty_bonus Exploration Reward for visiting states with high novelty (intrinsic motivation).
collision_penalty Penalty Negative reward for crashing into other units or map boundaries.
stagnation_penalty Penalty Negative reward for remaining idle without contributing.
overcrowding_penalty Penalty Negative reward for having too many units clustered in one zone.
Note on Data Logging: All Team and Reward KPIs are logged natively with step-by-step history arrays ([step_0, step_1, ..., step_750]), allowing for the rendering of progression curves in the interactive HTML dashboards.
