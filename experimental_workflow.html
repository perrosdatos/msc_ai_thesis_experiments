Figure 2: Detail of Phase 1

Phase 1: Reproducible Setup

Seed Generation
A master procedural seed (Seed = 42) initializes the generation of 50 globally unique map scenarios. This guarantees that all candidate models are evaluated under strictly identical spatial and environmental constraints, eliminating map layout variance.
generate_seeds.py -> seeds.csv
Checkpoint Aggregation
Trained network parameter states (Policies) for MAPPO, MASAC, and QMIX are aggregated from the training phase at designated frame intervals (e.g., Checkpoint 1, 20, 40) for longitudinal performance analysis.
models/**/*.pt
Figure 3: Detail of Phase 2

Phase 2: Dual Validation Pipeline

Cross-Play Sweep
A comprehensive head-to-head tournament evaluating relative algorithm performance across the 50 standardized scenarios.
Symmetric Matchup Enforcement

To mitigate any initial spawn location bias, each scenario is evaluated twice with swapped role assignments:
Model A (Team 0) vs Model B (Team 1)
AND
Model B (Team 0) vs Model A (Team 1)
1_run_cross_play_sweep.py
Baseline Tournament
An absolute performance evaluation tracking where candidate models are matched against a fixed environmental baseline.
Rule-Based Opponent

The baseline consists of a scripted agent executing a near-optimal heuristic exploitation strategy, representing the target task objective without stochastic variance.
3_evaluate_rulebased.py
Figure 4: Detail of Phase 3

Phase 3: Telemetry & Outputs

KPI Tracking
Execution of a dedicated telemetry module during inference to record:

- Win Margins & Team Points
- Map Exploration (%)
- Dispersion Variance
- Synergy Information Handoffs
- Mean Reward Components
kpi_tracker.py
Visual Inspection
Automatic rendering of the environmental state for the first seed (Seed 42) of every unique matchup configuration. Captured as MP4 video formats to facilitate qualitative behavioral analysis.
videos/*.mp4
Performance Reporting
Aggregation of evaluation tensors into statistical datasets. Produces CSV archives representing the complete episodic history and HTML dashboards summarizing statistical win rates and KPI distributions.
2_generate_visualizations.py
